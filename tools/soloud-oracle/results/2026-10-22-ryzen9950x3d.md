(10 = 44.1 and 48 kHz x 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 channels; max abs error 0 everywhere.)

Between platforms SoLoud itself differs only in `winddecay` at 48 kHz (all
channel counts): UCRT and glibc `cosf` round some wind filter arguments
differently; from frame 338 357 on, 1.7 % of samples differ, max abs 3.9e-7,
RMS 1.3e-8 at a peak of 0.27, energy relative 2e-9. Each port matches its own
platform's SoLoud bit for bit.

Without FTZ/DAZ in the port, `winddecay` differs in ~30 % of its samples
(max 2.9e-38: the denormal tail of the decaying wind gain); with it, identical.
