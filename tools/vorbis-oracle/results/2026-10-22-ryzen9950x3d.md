## Parity (vorbis-oracle compare, 6 trace modes per file)

Corpus from `scripts/gen_corpus.py` (1201 files, ~30 MB: 308 clean, 19 odd,
101 synthetic, 773 damaged) plus 31 public test vectors (FFmpeg FATE /
samples.ffmpeg.org, 13 of them floor 0; not committed).

| C library | corpus traces | vectors traces | identical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Windows: prebuilt 3p-ogg_vorbis v1.3.5-1.3.7-r2 (MSVC 14.42) | 7206 | 186 | all |
| Linux (WSL Ubuntu 26.04): same sources, 3p Linux flags, gcc 14.3 | 7206 | 186 | all |

Float sample hashes (packet modes) treat every NaN as one value: the C and
the port produce NaN at the same samples (malformed floor 0 data only) but
not always with the same sign bit; 16-bit `ov_read` output is unaffected.
